Privacy Policy — Range for Shopify

Last updated 9 September 2026

Range is a delivery marketplace in Lebanon. The Range for Shopify app lets a store owner show their Shopify catalog to Range shoppers and receive delivery orders through the Range Merchant app. This policy explains what the app reads from Shopify, what it keeps, and how to remove it.

What we access

  • Products and inventory (scopes read_products, read_inventory): product titles, descriptions, images, variants, prices and stock levels.
  • Store profile: the store name, its myshopify domain and currency, used to label the connection.

We do not request access to your customers, orders, payments, staff accounts or any other Shopify data. Shopify customer data never reaches Range through this app.

What we store

  • A copy of the imported products (name, description, image link, variants, price, stock) so shoppers can browse them in Range, plus any edits you make to them inside Range.
  • The Shopify access token for your store, stored server-side only and never shown to any app or person.
  • A log of the compliance webhooks Shopify sends us, to prove they were handled.

How we use it

Only to display your catalog to Range shoppers, keep prices and stock in sync when you re-import, and route Range orders to your store. We never sell, rent or share this data with third parties, and we never use it for advertising.

Sub-processors

Data is hosted on Supabase (database and serverless functions) and errors are monitored with Sentry. Both operate under their own data-processing terms.

Deletion

  • Uninstalling the app from Shopify revokes the access token immediately. Shopify then sends us a shop/redact request 48 hours later, and we erase the token and store profile and hide the imported catalog.
  • You can also ask us to delete everything at any time from Store → Support in the Range Merchant app or by email.
  • Because we hold no Shopify customer data, customers/data_request and customers/redact requests are acknowledged and logged with nothing to return or erase.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Every request from Shopify is verified with an HMAC signature. Access tokens are stored server-side only and are never sent to a phone or browser.
