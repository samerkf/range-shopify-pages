Range for Shopify — Support

Connecting takes about a minute: open the Range Merchant app → Store → Shopify → enter your .myshopify.com domain → tap Connect with Shopify → approve on Shopify's page. Your products import right away.

Common questions

  • Prices changed in Range but not in Shopify? Range keeps your Range edits; re-importing never overwrites them.
  • Removed a product in Shopify? It's hidden in Range on the next import.
  • Want to disconnect? Uninstall the app from your Shopify admin. Everything is erased within 48 hours.

Email support@range.app or message us from Store → Support in the app. We answer within one business day.
