Sell on Range with your Shopify catalog

Range is Lebanon's premium delivery marketplace. Install the Range app and your store's products appear in the Range shopper app, with delivery handled by Range drivers. We only read products and stock — never your customers or orders.

How it works

  1. Open the Range Merchant app → Store → Shopify.
  2. Enter your .myshopify.com domain and tap Connect with Shopify.
  3. Approve on Shopify's page. Your products import in seconds.

Prices you edit in Range stay yours; re-importing never overwrites them. Products removed in Shopify are hidden in Range on the next import.
